Jukebox Jam

Install firmware over USB — no software required

  1. Plug the device into this computer via USB-C.
  2. Click the button below and pick the device's serial port.
  3. Wait for the flash to finish, then the device reboots on its own straight into the Setup wizard.

Needs Chrome or Edge (Web Serial support). Firefox and Safari aren't supported.

After flashing, see the First Installation guide for joining it to your WiFi and Sonos speaker.
